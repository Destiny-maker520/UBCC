关于我们

大学商业咨询学会（UBCC）成立于 2009 年，是在香港政府注册的非营利性组织。自成立以来，UBCC 始终铭记创始人洪先生（David Hong）所强调的宗旨：“做靠谱的事，成为靠谱的人”。

我们的目标

我们一直致力于为学生提供实践专业知识和拓展咨询视野的平台。UBCC 为会员提供宏观经济、企业战略、金融市场等方面的专业培训，以及深入的分析和研究，与时俱进，帮助会员建立和巩固坚实的比较优势。

About Us

Founded in 2009, the University Business Consulting Club (UBCC) is a non-profit organization registered with the Hong Kong Government. Since its establishment, UBCC has always kept in mind the principle of "Doing reliable things, becoming reliable people" (as emphasized by the founder Mr. David Hong).

Our Aim

We have always been committed to providing a platform for students to practice professional knowledge and expand consulting vision. UBCC offers our members specialized training in macroeconomics, corporate strategies and financial markets, in-depth analysis and research, keeping pace with the development of the times, as well as establishing and cultivating a solid comparative advantage.
